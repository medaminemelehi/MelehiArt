The Artist
Biography

Born in Asilah,
Shaped by Morocco

"His work navigates between structure and spontaneity, creating a balance where imagination unfolds freely."

Born in 1953 in the picturesque coastal town of Asilah, Mohamed Amine Melehi dedicated decades to the plastic arts — teaching, creating, and exhibiting across Morocco and around the globe. He studied at the prestigious School of Arts and Architecture of Marseille, and returned home to leave a lasting mark on Moroccan cultural life.

Published Work

101 Moroccan
Folk Tales

Discover one hundred and one traditional Moroccan folk tales, lovingly collected and arranged in Asilah by Mohamed Amine Melehi. A treasure trove of coastal and Moroccan heritage, bridging generations through the art of storytelling.

Artist · Ceramist · Educator

Mohamed

Born in 1953 in the picturesque coastal town of Asilah, Morocco. With decades of artistic practice, teaching, and international exhibitions, his work has become an enduring part of Moroccan cultural heritage.
